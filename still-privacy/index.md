# Privacy Policy — Still

Last updated: 25 July 2026

Still (“the App”) compresses photos and videos locally on your device. Your selected media is not uploaded to our servers.

## Media and on-device data

- **Selected media:** Photos and videos are processed locally for compression.
- **Metadata:** EXIF and color-profile metadata may be preserved or removed according to your settings.
- **App state:** Preferences, Pro entitlement state, free-output balance, and rewarded-ad history are stored on your device.

Still does not require an account and does not receive your payment-card details.

## Optional rewarded advertising

Free users may voluntarily watch a rewarded advertisement to receive additional output credits. Still uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google’s User Messaging Platform for advertising and privacy choices.

Depending on your region, device settings, consent choices, and Google’s configuration, Google may process:

- IP address and approximate location derived from it;
- device or app identifiers, including the advertising identifier when permitted;
- advertising data and interactions, such as ad impressions and video views;
- diagnostic, crash, and performance information.

This information may be used to deliver and measure ads, prevent abuse, and improve advertising services. Still never sends the photo or video you are compressing to Google for advertising.

## App campaign measurement

Still includes Meta App Events from the Facebook iOS SDK to support measurement of Still app-install and re-engagement campaigns. Automatic event logging, codeless event logging, and advertising-identifier collection are disabled by default.

When campaign measurement is enabled after the applicable privacy choice, Meta may process limited app and device information, IP-derived approximate location, diagnostics, and fixed app events such as app activation, successful output, rewarded-ad completion, or a verified Pro purchase. Still does not send selected media, photo-library identifiers, filenames, file paths, album names, or compression metadata to Meta.

## Consent and your choices

- Rewarded ads are optional. You can use the daily free allowance or Still Pro without watching an ad.
- Where required, the App asks for advertising consent before requesting an ad.
- When applicable, you can reopen your choices from **Settings → Privacy choices**.
- After the applicable advertising-consent flow, iOS may ask whether you allow tracking. Declining does not block compression, Pro features, or rewarded ads; Still does not enable advertising-identifier collection unless you allow it.
- You can revoke Photos access in **iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos**.

## Purchases

In-App Purchases are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

## Retention

App preferences and local working data remain on your device until removed by you, the App’s retention controls, or deletion of the App. Third-party processing is governed by the provider’s own retention and privacy terms.
